Yankee Institute / Local design review

A clearer, more approachable Yankee.

Concepts 1 and 3 were reset after feedback, using Empower Mississippi's bold type, generous spacing and welcoming institutional hierarchy as the visual reference. Both retain Yankee's identity, assigned headlines and shared verified content.

Concept 1 / Revised after reference feedback

Open Connecticut

A people-focused introduction, readable Figtree typography, large story photography and a full-width South Norwalk chapter. Recent reporting stays immediately below the opening, with issue paths connecting analysis, research and testimony.

Best at: welcoming residents and introducing Yankee's work quickly.

Trade-offs: the family photo is illustrative stock; desktop article summaries are condensed to keep five stories visible. The complete collection follows below.

Concept 3 / Revised after reference feedback

Connecticut Forward

A confident blue brand opening with a three-window Hartford panorama. Clear subject paths connect everyday concerns to the underlying policy record. Figtree replaces the rejected Syne display face. Rich photography carries through story cards, policy tiles and major research.

Best at: a strong Connecticut identity and topic-led discovery.

Trade-offs: the darker opening is more assertive. Some subjects have reporting but no research report in this 22-piece sample, and the interface identifies that distinction.

Concept 2 / Active refinement

Connecticut Edition

The photographic publication direction, selected for further refinement. A mission introduction and four photographic core-issue cards now follow the full-bleed opening, ahead of Latest. A held introduction and layered photographic work examples connect the commitments to real policy activity. Traditional navigation and a policy mega menu make mission and content easy to reach. Visual approval is pending.

Best at: immersive connection to place.

Trade-offs: a longer opening and a more editorial tone than the supplied reference.
