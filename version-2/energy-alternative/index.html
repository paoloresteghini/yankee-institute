The power to thrive.

Energy policy reaches into family budgets and business decisions. Yankee Institute advocates practical choices that make costs easier to understand, measure programs by their results and keep dependable power central to Connecticut’s future.

Make every charge clear.

Ratepayers should be able to understand the energy charges, public benefits charges and other surcharges on their bills, and what those charges fund.

Evaluate mandates on cost and performance.

Review energy programs against clear standards for cost and intended results. Ratepayers deserve an honest account of what a mandate achieves.

Keep affordability and reliability together.

Build dependable power and manageable costs into energy policy from the start. Families and businesses need a grid they can count on.

Consider every carbon-free source.

Recognize nuclear power alongside other carbon-free technologies when evaluating energy policy. Judge solutions by their performance and contribution to reliable supply.
