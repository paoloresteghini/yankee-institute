Home/About

Improving lives through freedom and opportunity.

Connecticut should be a place where you can work, retire and see your children prosper. We advance free-market, limited-government solutions to help make that possible.

Meet our team ↓

A brighter future.
A practical purpose.

We believe Connecticut needs smart, limited government, fairness for taxpayers and an open road to opportunity.

Yankee Institute develops policy ideas and helps Connecticut residents understand the decisions shaping their lives. Our work brings research, citizen education and advocacy together.

Explore our research ↗

Yankee Institute is a 501(c)(3) research and citizen education organization. We do not accept government funding.

For the place
we call home.

Our focus is a Connecticut where opportunity reaches more people.

  • A stronger economy and job market.
  • A more affordable life for young people, families and retirees.
  • A better balance between citizens and government workers.
  • An environment where all businesses can grow.
